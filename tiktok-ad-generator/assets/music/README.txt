OPTIONAL BACKGROUND MUSIC
=========================

Drop ONE music file in this folder (mp3, m4a, wav, aac or ogg) and tick
"Add background music" in the app. It will be mixed quietly under the voice.

IMPORTANT — use music you're allowed to use:
  * Free / royalty-free music: Pixabay Music, YouTube Audio Library, Mixkit.
  * Do NOT put copyrighted songs here if you plan to monetize.

BEST TIP FOR TIKTOK:
  Leave this empty and instead add a TRENDING SOUND inside the TikTok app
  after you upload the video. Trending sounds get you more reach, and the
  app keeps your spoken voiceover anyway. You can lower the original sound
  and raise the trending track right in TikTok.
